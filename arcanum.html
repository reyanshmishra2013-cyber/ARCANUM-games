The Spheres Await

A primer on the rites before you

How to Play

Flip two cards at a time to reveal the celestial sigils hidden within. Match a pair of identical symbols to clear them from the board. Clear every pair to complete the rite. Consecutive matches build a streak — and with it, the spheres sing louder.

The Rites — Game Modes

The VigilThe classic rite. Match at your own pace with no external pressure.
Chronomancer's RiteA countdown hangs over every move. Clear the board before the hourglass runs dry.
Umbral TrialYou carry three sigils. Each miss shatters one. Lose all three and the trial ends.
Fate's GambitThe oracle grants a limited number of moves. Spend them wisely or be undone.
Shifting VeilUnclaimed sigils quietly trade places every so often. Trust your memory, not the map.

Sigils & The Chronicle

New card themes and the story of your journey — games played, win rate, and the achievements you've earned — are unlocked as you play. Open Chronicle from the header at any time to see your progress.

The Paths — Difficulty

Archmage · 6×6Eighteen pairs. Only a true master of memory prevails.
