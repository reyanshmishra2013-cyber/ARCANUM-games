HOW TO PLAY

🕹️ Move the paddle with your mouse, finger, or arrow keys.
🚀 Launch the ball with a click, tap, or SPACE.
💥 Break every brick to clear the level. The ball speeds up as you clear!
⚡ Power-ups: Wide, Slow, Life, Fireball, Shield, Multi-ball, Coins, Laser (⌖), Sticky (⊓), Big Ball (●), Bomb (💣), Freeze (❄), Score x2, Slow-Field (◈).
☠️ Curse drops (red) shrink you, speed the ball, or reverse controls — dodge them!
👹 Every 6th level is a BOSS with 3 escalating phases — it fires more shots as its HP drops. Dodge!
🔥 Tap/click while Laser is active to blast bricks. Sticky paddle holds the ball for aimed launches.
🎯 Chain bricks without touching the paddle for TRICK SHOT bonus points!
👥 Hit 2P CO-OP to play Endless with a friend — P1 uses mouse/A-D, P2 uses arrow keys.
☠️ Toggle HARDCORE (top-right) for brutal speed, tougher bosses & more hazards.
🛠️ In the LEVEL EDITOR, build your own stage, add unbreakable WALLS to make it harder, and hit PREVIEW to test the bounce before you play!
🎵 Buy alternate soundtracks in the shop. 🏆 Earn coins, climb the leaderboards, and chase achievements!
